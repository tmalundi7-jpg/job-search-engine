## Target Roles (Based on the Exhaustive Career Guide)
Do NOT apply or search for CFO, Finance Director, Financial Controller, or Audit Partner roles. The candidate is junior/mid-level. Focus ONLY on roles that realistically fit 1-3 years of experience and CIMA studies:

1. **Management Accounting Track (Industry):**
   - Entry/Mid Management Accountant
   - Assistant Management Accountant
   - Cost Accountant
   *Salary expectation (Regional): £25,000 - £45,000*

2. **FP&A and Commercial Finance Track (Industry):**
   - Finance Analyst / Commercial Analyst
   - Junior FP&A Analyst
   *Salary expectation (Regional): £28,000 - £45,000*

3. **Public Sector Track (NHS / Local Government / Education):**
   - Finance Officer
   - Finance Assistant (Senior level)
   - Assistant Management Accountant (NHS Band 5 or 6)
   *Salary expectation: £25,000 - £40,000*

## Roles to EXCLUDE
- **External Audit / Big 4 Practice roles** (Candidate is CIMA, which is industry-focused, not ACA/ACCA practice-focused).
- **Tax roles** (Requires CTA or specific tax experience which candidate lacks).
- **Senior/Executive roles** requiring 5+ years of post-qualification experience. 

## Instructions for Agents
When scoring and scraping, immediately discard any job that requires the candidate to be "fully qualified" (unless it explicitly says "or part-qualified"). Discard jobs located in London unless they are 100% remote. Heavily weight jobs in Scotland, specifically mentioning CIMA study support, management accounts, and Excel skills.
